        Not missable — everything else

        Every other trophy can still be earned after the story in free-roam. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, but all trophy-related quest givers, activities and collectibles stay available. Optional missions that disappear (black icons with pink letters) do not matter for trophies, and there are far more than 100 optional missions for the 100% checklist. Save advice: keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — 15 manual slots — so you always have a way back.

        Missable verdict: PowerPyx (18 Nov 2026) flags Positive Mental Attitude as the only potentially missable trophy; GTABase agrees. The Chapter 1 meditation's role is still being confirmed, so this guide tells you to do it rather than risk it.
